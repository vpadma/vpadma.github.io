Diagram notes & learning references

Original, simplified anatomical schematics; vessels are separated and some paired structures are shown on only one side for clarity. These are orientation and pathway aids, not dissection photographs or scale drawings. Posterior structures may be projected onto the whole-body view; dedicated posterior views are labeled.

The first tab includes all 30 terms under the PDF’s “Principal Arteries of the Human Body.” The PDF typo “External Carotid Srtery” is corrected. “Arcuate artery” is interpreted as the foot vessel; “phrenic vein” is shown as inferior phrenic. Confirm these two terms against your lab model.
